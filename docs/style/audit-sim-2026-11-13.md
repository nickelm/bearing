# Simulation view style audit, 2026-11-13

V1's style audit: the simulation view's previews read against Part I of `docs/visual-style.md`. Seed 1983, 1024 × 576, rendered headless on the software Vulkan driver, after decisions 123–128.

**Method.** I rendered every simulation preview (`VIEWS=checks` and the feature views `trees`, `town`, `sunset`, `night`, `winter`, `sea`, `shore`, `lake`, `tower`, `farmland`, `alpine`) and compared each with Part I's text and with the written takeaways of the five references. The reference screenshots are local only (decision 70) and were not in the cloud container, so the side-by-side comparison with the board is still the owner's.

**Verdicts.** _Match_: the views show what Part I asks. _Partial_: the feature is there but falls short somewhere. _Gap_: missing or wrong. Every gap and partial has a TODO task.

## What to avoid

None of Part I's list shows: no smooth shading on close terrain since decision 123, no photographic textures, no saturated primaries outside lights, no cel bands or terrain outlines, no uniform tree scatter (forests come in clusters with edges).

## Fixes to the views made during the audit

`sea` and `shore` used fixed offsets from the main continent's center, which land on dry ground in this world; they now look at the check-5 coast, into the sun and with the sun behind. `winter` looked at the check-1 town near the equator, where midwinter brings no snow; it now looks at the check-2 forest edge on its hemisphere's midwinter day (`midwinter`). `town` turns on trees and buildings.
