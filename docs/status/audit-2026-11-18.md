# Project Audit, 2026-11-18

Bearing is a cartoony browser game; the values and systems here serve gameplay (README.md, What Bearing Is; CLAUDE.md rule 22).

_Audit of `main` at `f020d6d` (decision 227) for Proposal 0008 (`docs/proposals/0008-game-loop-and-campaign.md`). The code is the ground truth for what exists. The audit read every proposal, plan, report and the decision log, and checked each claim against `src/` and `tests/`. `docs/roadmap.md` turns the findings into phases R0–R6._

## Summary

Bearing has a world and a flyable aircraft. It does not yet have a war that fights.

- **World generation is mature.** The coarse pass, the eroded global pass, rivers with migration and oxbows, provinces, rock, twenty biomes, zones, wonders, the strategic graph with settlements, roads, railways and typed bridges, theater detection, casting, and the alignment of every road and railway all run, are tested, and are cached with the world in IndexedDB. Over seeds 1–20 on generator version 34, 19 worlds have a continental front.
- **Both views render.** The strategic map has relief, hillshade, patterns, glyphs, labels and military overlays. The simulation view has faceted terrain with LOD, forests for low flight, sky, volumetric weather, water and post-processing.
- **Flight exists only in the Free Flight sandbox.** The Kestrel flies a tested, bit-pinned fixed-wing model; two helicopters fly a rotor-disc model under the player's hands. The HUD, the display system with five formats, the status strip, master modes, the sensor model and avionics profiles for all 31 types exist. All of it lives in `src/freeflight/` on the main thread.
- **The campaign's simulation layer plans and executes, but never resolves.** The sim worker runs an interim hourly tasker and analytic flights at up to 256×. No mission fails, nothing is destroyed, no pilot ejects, the front never moves, and no ground or naval aggregates exist. The player cannot search, follow, take over or hand back a mission. The campaign has no snapshot, save or event log.

The tech demo's gap is therefore the middle of the loop (resolution, consequence, ground war) and the bridge between the two halves (full fidelity for campaign aircraft). Weapons, ejection and CSAR start from zero.

## Decisions for the Owner

Each lists options and a recommendation. The roadmap follows the recommendation until the owner decides otherwise; decision 231 records the ones taken provisionally.

**The owner's answers (2026-11-18, decision 232):** every recommendation is accepted except decision 2, where the owner chose (b): each aircraft type becomes one JSON bundle, and the avionics profiles and air-war entries move out of code into the specs.

1. **Where full-fidelity flight runs.** (a) The flown or followed aircraft steps on the main thread at 120 Hz, as in Free Flight, and its session's consequences (position, fuel, stores, damage, kills, ejections) return to the worker as logged campaign events at handback; (b) every full-fidelity aircraft runs in the sim worker, with the player's inputs posted per step. **Recommendation: (a).** It keeps input latency at zero and reuses Free Flight; replay reproduces the campaign from the logged consequences without re-running physics, which meets proposal 0008's replay criterion. Revisit (b) for co-op.
2. **Aircraft type bundle.** (a) One type registry keyed by type id joins the air-war table, the avionics profile, the JSON spec (model, flight data, pylons, rig) and the calibration table; (b) move everything into the JSON specs. **Recommendation: (a)** for R2; most types have no spec yet, and decision 224 placed profiles in code. Proposal 0008's intent (one place per type) is met by the registry and a completeness test.
3. **Helicopters in the demo.** Proposal 0008 needs AI-only Black Hawk, Mi-24 and Mi-8 analogues, and CSAR needs a rescue helicopter; no rotary director exists, and decision 151 kept new helicopter types out of the campaign. (a) Pull a rotary director, an AI helicopter pilot (route, approach, landing at a point) and a utility helicopter role into R2, accepting one change to every world's cast; (b) followed AI helicopters fly analytic routes in R2. **Recommendation: (a)**, AI only, with the Assisted laws; player-flown campaign helicopters, hazards and sling loads stay in R4.
4. **Name clash.** The UH-68 "Buzzard" helicopter and the A-15 Buzzard (A-10 analogue). **Recommendation:** rename the helicopter.
